Privacy policy

ChartJam · last updated 22 November 2026

In short

  • Your songs, setlists, styles and settings stay on your device. We don't receive them.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. A cookie-free counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics) counts page visits, without a profile of you.
  • To check a Pro subscription, the app sends your licence key to Polar, who sold it to you.
  • The tuner uses the microphone, only while it's listening, to measure the note you play on your device. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent.
  • When you share a song, setlist or collection, it goes as a file or link through the app you choose to whoever you send it to. A song link carries the song itself; it never passes through a server of ours.

Who is responsible

MC, 8500 Kortrijk, Belgium, is responsible for this app.

What stays on your device

The library (songs, collections, setlists, your own styles) is stored in your browser's database (IndexedDB), and your settings and licence key in its local storage, with a key the app makes on your device to seal the licence against tampering (it can't be read out and never leaves the device). The app needs these to work; they are not sent to us. You delete them with the app's Clear button, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Microphone. The tuner (an experimental feature, off unless you switch it on) asks your browser for the microphone when you start it, and uses it only while it's listening: the sound is measured on your device, a few hundredths of a second at a time, to show the note you play, then dropped. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can withdraw the permission in your browser's site settings at any time.

Recordings. Record yourself (an experimental feature) records the microphone only while you record a take, from pressing Record until playback stops. The takes are kept in your browser's database on this device (the last few of each song), are not in backups or shared songs, and leave the device only if you export one yourself.

What reaches other parties

  • Sharing. Only when you press Share or Export: the file, or a song's link, goes through the app you pick (mail, a chat, AirDrop, Nearby Sharing…) to the people you send it to, under that app's own terms. A song link holds the song in the part after the #, which browsers never send to a web server, so opening it doesn't send the song to us or to GitHub; anyone who has the link or the file can open the song, so share it as you would the song itself.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, it receives your IP address and browser details when pages load, and may keep them in its logs for security.
  • Visit counting. To know how many people use the site and how they find it, each page load sends Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) the page's address, the page you came from, your browser and device type and your IP address (for the country). It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, doesn't follow you across visits or sites, and Cloudflare doesn't use it for advertising; we see only totals. It isn't loaded on a shared song link's page, so a song in a link is never sent. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). A browser's tracking protection or an ad blocker may stop it, which is fine.
  • Instrument sounds. The app loads its sound samples from other public sites (GitHub Pages sites of the sample libraries credited in the legal notice), which likewise receive your IP address. The app keeps the samples for offline use, so this happens once per sound.
  • Subscriptions. When you subscribe, you pay at Polar (polar.sh), our reseller and merchant of record. Polar processes your name, email address, billing details and payment as the seller, under its own privacy policy on polar.sh. The app itself never sees your payment details.
  • The licence check. With a licence key, the app sends the key, a name for your device (e.g. "Windows · 2026-11-11") and your IP address to Polar, to unlock Pro and to check, about once a day when you're online, that your subscription is still active. Legal basis: carrying out your subscription (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). From Polar we learn which subscriptions and devices are active, linked to the email address you gave Polar.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your message and address to answer you, and as long as needed for your question or our legal duties.

Some of these parties are in the USA; they transfer data under the EU's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

The app sets no cookies, and neither does the visit counter. It uses your browser's storage only for what it needs to work (above), which needs no consent. Polar's checkout, on Polar's own site, has its own cookie notice.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to limit its use or object to it, and ask for a copy (data portability), by writing to us (our contact address follows soon). For your purchase data, you can also go to Polar directly. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
