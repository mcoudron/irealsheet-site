What stays on your device

The library (songs, collections, setlists, your own styles) is stored in your browser's database (IndexedDB), and your settings and licence key in its local storage. The app needs these to work; they are not sent to us. You delete them with the app's Clear button, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

What reaches other parties

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, it receives your IP address and browser details when pages load, and may keep them in its logs for security.
  • Instrument sounds. The app loads its sound samples from other public sites (GitHub Pages sites of the sample libraries credited in the legal notice), which likewise receive your IP address. The app keeps the samples for offline use, so this happens once per sound.
  • Subscriptions. When you subscribe, you pay at Polar (polar.sh), our reseller and merchant of record. Polar processes your name, email address, billing details and payment as the seller, under its own privacy policy on polar.sh. The app itself never sees your payment details.
  • The licence check. With a licence key, the app sends the key, a name for your device (e.g. "Windows · 2026-11-11") and your IP address to Polar, to unlock Pro and to check, about once a day when you're online, that your subscription is still active. Legal basis: carrying out your subscription (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). From Polar we learn which subscriptions and devices are active, linked to the email address you gave Polar.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your message and address to answer you, and as long as needed for your question or our legal duties.

Some of these parties are in the USA; they transfer data under the EU's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies

The app sets no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only for what it needs to work (above), which needs no consent. Polar's checkout, on Polar's own site, has its own cookie notice.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to limit its use or object to it, and ask for a copy (data portability), by writing to support@chartjam.app. For your purchase data, you can also go to Polar directly. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
