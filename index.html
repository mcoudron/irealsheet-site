About ChartJam

ChartJam is a practice band in your browser: open a lead sheet, choose a key and a style, and play along with piano, bass, guitar and drums.

Backing tracks to practise with

Swing, bossa nova, blues, ballads and more, at any tempo, with a count-in, repeats and a cursor that follows the chart.

Lead sheets in any key

Transpose a chart in one tap, or read it for your instrument: B♭ trumpet or tenor sax, E♭ alto sax, F horn, bass clef for trombone.

Practice tools

Ear training with the melody hidden, a speed trainer, the tune in every key, loops of a few bars and a tuner.

For bands and gigs

Setlists with a key and tempo per gig, stage mode with page turns by foot pedal, and songs shared with a link.
