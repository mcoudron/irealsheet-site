Getting started

›What is ChartJam?

A practice app for musicians: it shows lead sheets (melody, chords and form) and plays a band behind them, piano, bass, guitar and drums in the style you choose, in any key and at any tempo. It runs in your browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.

›Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Your songs and settings stay on your device.

›Can I install it like an app?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge choose "Install app" in the address bar or menu; on an iPhone or iPad, tap Share and "Add to Home Screen". It then opens in its own window and works offline.

›Does it work offline?

Yes, once a page and its sounds have loaded while online. Each instrument's sounds download the first time they play and are kept, so play a song with your usual style and melody instrument once before you go offline.

Songs and files

›Where do songs come from?

From ABC files, a common text format for lead sheets. Import one with the file button on the home page: a single tune, or a tunebook with many tunes. You can also write a new song with the + button, in the score editor or as ABC text.

›Why isn't my favourite standard included?

Most standards are protected by copyright: their melodies and lyrics can't be shared without the publishers' permission. We only include songs in the public domain and our own practice material. Charts you write or import yourself are yours to use.

›How do I keep my songs safe, or move them to another device?

Your library lives only in this browser on this device. Use the download button on the home page to save a backup file now and then, and the upload button to restore it, on the same device or another one. Clearing the browser's data for the site deletes the library, so keep a backup.

›Can I group songs, or make a setlist for a gig?

Yes. Collections group songs (e.g. per band); the chips above the library filter on them. Setlists, behind the list icon on the home page, put songs in gig order with a key, tempo and note per song; playing a setlist steps through it with the previous and next buttons.

Playing

›How do I choose the style, tempo and count-in?

In the player's side panel (on a phone, the controls button): Backing Style, and in the Playback card the tempo, the count-in and how many times the song repeats. Each song remembers its own settings.

›Can I hear the melody?

Yes: switch Melody on in the Mixer and pick its sound (piano, a horn, woodwind, vibes, Rhodes, organ…). The mixer also sets the level of each part of the band.

›How do I start from a certain bar, or loop a few bars?

Stopped or paused, tap a bar and Play starts there; while playing, double-tap a bar to jump to it. To loop, press the loop button next to Play, then tap the first and the last bar of the loop.

›The highlight runs ahead of or behind what I hear.

With Bluetooth headphones or speakers the sound arrives later. Set Bluetooth Delay in the Playback card until the highlight matches the sound.

Keys and instruments

›How do I change the key?

Pick another key in the Key menu. The chart and the band both move to the new key.

›I play trumpet, sax or horn. Can I see my part?

Yes: choose your instrument in the Instrument menu. The chart is written for it (B♭, E♭ or F, or bass clef for trombone), fitted into its range, and the Key menu shows the key as you read it, with the concert key in brackets. The band always plays in concert pitch, so you can play along as you read.

Practice tools

›What is ear training?

In the Mixer: the notes of the melody are hidden while it plays, so you learn the tune by ear and still see the chords and form.

›What does the speed trainer do?

In the Playback card: the song loops and gets a little faster each time round, from a start tempo up to an end tempo, so you can work a tune up to speed.

›Can I hide the lyrics?

Yes: for a song with lyrics, the Lyrics switch under Instrument hides them. The song itself isn't changed.

›Can I make my own backing style?

Yes, behind the styles icon on the home page: start a new style or copy a built-in one, and set the drums, bass, piano and guitar hits bar by bar. Your own styles appear in the Backing Style menu.

Editing and exporting

›How do I fix a wrong note or chord?

Open the editor with the pencil button in the player. In the Score tab, tap a note to select it and change it with the tools or the keyboard (the ? button lists the shortcuts); the ABC text tab edits the text directly.

›Can I print a chart?

Yes: export it as a PDF or a PNG image with the buttons at the bottom of the player's panel.

Privacy and help

›What do you know about me?

Next to nothing: no account, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Your library stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

›I don't hear anything.

Turn the volume up and check the right output is chosen; on an iPhone, the silent switch can mute the app, so switch it off. Browsers only start sound after a tap, so press Play yourself. The first time, the sounds still have to download, which takes a moment on a slow connection.

›Something's wrong, or I have an idea.

Write to support@chartjam.app. We read everything.
