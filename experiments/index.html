Experimental features

Features still being worked on. Each is off until you switch it on here, on this device, and may still change or go away.

  • Chord charts

    Type only the chords and the form (bars, repeats, endings, sections, D.S. and Coda, dynamics) and get a chart in slash notation that plays at once: the home page's + then offers Chord chart next to the score editor, and editing a chord chart opens its chords as text. Free.

  • Tuner

    A tuning fork button in the player opens a tuner that listens to the note you play (through the microphone, nothing recorded or sent) and a reference tone to tune to, both in the written pitch of your instrument, with A = 440 or another reference pitch. Free.

  • Feel controls

    A Feel section in the player's Playback card: how far the band swings, whether it plays ahead of the beat, on it or laid back, and how much the piano, guitar and drums play. Saved per song. Pro.

  • Harmonic analysis

    In the player's Training card: under every chord, what it does in the key, as Roman numerals (ii7, V7, Imaj7, V7/ii, subV7) or Nashville numbers (2m7, 5⁷), a bracket under each ii–V and the key where a ii–V–I moves for a moment (→ F). The same in every key and for every instrument, and in the PDF. Pro.

  • Record yourself

    A red Record button in the player records you over the band, from the count-in to the end; a Takes card keeps each song's last 5 takes (starred ones stay) to listen back to. On this device only. Pro.
